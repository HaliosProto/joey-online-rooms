Install & how to play

On iPhone, open in Safari, then Share → Add to Home Screen. On Android, use Install app or Add to Home screen. This version runs directly on your device.

Both friends must use Joey Fast. Create a room or join one below. Tap a card to inspect it and choose an action. The game handles card effects and legal moves.

Keep the app open during an online duel. Leaving or losing the connection ends the room. Practice lets you control both players on one device.

This is a new client using a classic five-zone field and modern card-effect scripts. It does not connect to the old Windows game. Campaign progress and saves from the original game are separate.
